\item Notes: All entries are annualized percentage points. The survey-based expectation-hypothesis proxy is the observed ten-year nominal yield minus the observed ten-year real yield and the ten-year CPI forecast. The fitted-observable proxy replaces those three inputs with their fitted values. The model premium compares log certainty-equivalent values of cumulative inflation under the risk-neutral and physical measures. Their difference is a definition and convexity wedge generated by Jensen, covariance, and nonlinear lower-bound terms; it is not a fitting error. The fitted-observable error equals exactly the sum of the three fit contributions; their RMSEs do not add because the contributions covary. The columns contain 139 and 91 survey dates, respectively.
