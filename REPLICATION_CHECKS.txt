REPLICATION CHECKS — 25 NOVEMBER 2026 — PTR20 ADOPTION

Application: February 1992–August 2026, 415 monthly observations, 30 free
coordinates. PTR measurement-error SD is fixed at 0.20 annualized percentage
point. Additional CPI measurement disturbance is zero; the economic transitory
inflation shock remains. Four empirical moment targets and the other fixed
measurement-error calibrations are held fixed for conditional inference.

The portable Rcpp/RcppArmadillo filter reproduces the independent R filter
and the adopted empirical point. Two independent finite-difference checks of
the original IEKF criterion give maximum normalized scores around 2e-6 with
no lower-bound formula switches. The full negative Hessian is positive definite
at the documented linearly rescaled differentiation steps; no eigenvalues or
directions are removed. Full-Jacobian White and 12-lag Bartlett/HAC covariances,
sum/mean normalization, Toeplitz reconstruction and step-size checks pass.
The half-step parameter-SE comparison has a maximum change of about 1.2%.

State/premium bands combine conditional filtering variance with the full
30-coordinate parameter covariance using a local delta approximation. Independent
spectral variance reconstruction, pricing gradients and a derivative half-step
check pass. These are pointwise local normal approximations conditional on the
empirical moments and calibrations, not finite-sample coverage guarantees.

current parameters and observation masks, with independent R/C++ checks.
